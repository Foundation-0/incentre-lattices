**Subject:** Two questions on a periodic framework of bridged octahedra (CaB6 boron net)

Dear colleague,

I am writing with two specific questions about a periodic bar framework: regular cross-polytopes, one per cubic cell, joined by one bar per axis. In three dimensions it is the boron net of CaB6 (RCSR cab, sphere-packing type 5/3/c3).

1. Is a contact-preserving continuous motion of the CaB6 boron sphere packing to fcc already known in the sphere-packing deformation or deployment literature? Our motion keeps every bar, keeps the octahedra in a fixed orientation while the lattice deforms, and has strictly increasing density.
2. For unit springs on all bars of this framework in dimension n, are C11/C12 = (n-1)^2 and nu_100 = 1/(n^2-n-1) known, or immediate from standard periodic-framework elasticity?

A pointer to a reference, or a short "this is routine", would help us a great deal. We make no claim of priority.

- Repository: https://github.com/aska-digital/incentre-lattice
- Preprint, version 2: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23340326
- Motion: https://github.com/aska-digital/incentre-lattice/tree/main/results/fold-continuous-fcc
- Elasticity: https://github.com/aska-digital/incentre-lattice/tree/main/results/rigidity-nd

Disclosure: most of the proofs, code and writing were produced with AI models (Claude from Anthropic; some parts with OpenAI Codex/GPT) under my direction, with internal review only.
